Still Rosary Support

Privacy Policy

Still Rosary is an independent English-language Catholic rosary app for iPhone and iPad (iOS or iPadOS 17 or later) and Apple Watch (watchOS 10 or later). It is not affiliated with a church or diocese.

Pray and resume

In Pray, choose Guided for the words of each prayer, or Beads to count prayers at your own pace. Choose a set of mysteries and tap Begin the Rosary. Close a prayer to save your place, then tap Resume rosary when you’re ready. To look up prayers or mysteries without starting or changing your rosary, tap the book in the Pray toolbar.

Verses and reminders

The Verse of the Day rotates by local date. It is not the Mass reading for the day. You can bookmark it, share it as text or an image, or add the Still Rosary widget to your Home Screen. Saved verses stay on that device.

To set a daily reminder, go to Settings → Prayer → Prayer Reminder, allow notifications, and set Daily at. To stop it, turn the setting off. If an enabled reminder is silent, check iOS notification settings and any active Focus.

Journal, iCloud, and Apple Watch

A dot in the Journal calendar marks a day on which you completed a rosary. The Records view shows your completed rosaries as a list. A day without a dot is not a missed goal.

On iPhone and iPad, you can opt in to sync completed rosaries through the same Apple Account. Intentions, unfinished progress, and saved verses do not sync. The Apple Watch app can start and save a rosary without your iPhone; its Journal and progress are separate.

Get help

Email hantmac@outlook.com. Include your device model, system version, and what you were doing when the problem occurred. Please leave out private intentions and exported records.
