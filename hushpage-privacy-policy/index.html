Hushpage Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Last updated / 最后更新：2026-11-11

English · 简体中文 · Support / 技术支持

English

1. Scope and developer

Hushpage (Hushpage: Headache Diary) is a headache diary for iPhone and Apple Watch, developed by Shanjie Han. It includes Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, Control Center controls, and Siri and Shortcuts actions. This policy covers all of them. This page does not indicate App Review approval or App Store availability.

Privacy and support contact: hantmac@outlook.com.

2. Summary: the developer collects no data

  • Hushpage has no account, no sign-in and no server. Nothing you log is sent to the developer.
  • It contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, no crash-reporting SDK and no tracking of any kind.
  • Everything you record stays on your devices unless you choose to share it.

3. What the app stores, and where

  • Your entries. Each entry is a time, a headache level (mild, moderate or severe) or a “took medication” mark, and an optional note you type. They are stored in a file inside the app’s own container on your iPhone, shared only with Hushpage’s widgets and controls on the same iPhone so they can show and log entries.
  • Settings. Preferences such as whether Apple Health saving is on, the report period and the optional name you type for reports are stored locally.
  • Apple Watch. If you use the Watch app, recent entries are copied between your paired iPhone and Apple Watch through Apple’s WatchConnectivity framework. This transfer is directly between your own devices; it does not pass through any server run by the developer.
  • Backups. Like other app data, your entries can be included in your iPhone’s iCloud or computer backups, depending on your own backup settings. Apple handles those backups under its own policy.

4. Apple Health (optional, off by default)

If you turn on “Save to Apple Health”, Hushpage asks for permission to write Headache samples (with the level you chose) to the Health app. Hushpage requests no permission to read any Health data and never reads it. Data written to Health is managed by Apple and by you in the Health app; you can delete it there or revoke Hushpage’s access at any time in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices. Health data is never used for advertising or shared with anyone by the developer.

5. Reports and exports

The doctor report (PDF) and CSV export are created on your iPhone. They leave the device only when you choose to share them, for example by AirDrop, Mail, Messages or saving to Files. Whoever you share them with, and the app or service you use to share, then handles that copy under their own terms.

6. Siri, Shortcuts, widgets and controls

Logging from Siri, Shortcuts, the Action button, widgets or Control Center runs Hushpage’s own actions on your device and writes to the same local entries. Requests you speak to Siri are processed by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

7. Purchases

The one-time “Doctor Reports” purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer receives no payment details. The app checks your purchase status with StoreKit on your device to unlock reports.

8. Support email and this website

If you email support, we receive what you choose to send (for example your email address, your description of the problem, app and iOS versions, and any attachments) and use it only to reply and fix problems. Please do not send screenshots that show health details you’d rather keep private.

This policy and the support page are hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting these pages makes ordinary web requests that may create connection logs held by the host. This is separate from the app, which does not open this website on its own.

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Privacy Statement

9. Deleting your data

  • Delete individual entries in the app: tap a day, then swipe an entry or open it and tap Delete Entry.
  • Delete everything by deleting the app from your iPhone and Apple Watch. This removes the entries stored by the app; copies in your own backups follow your backup settings.
  • Headache samples saved to Apple Health stay in the Health app until you delete them there.
  • There is no account and no server copy, so there is nothing for the developer to delete. To ask about support emails you sent us, contact hantmac@outlook.com.

10. Health information and medical use

Hushpage is a personal diary, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice. The information you record is yours; decide for yourself who you share it with.

11. Children

Hushpage is not directed at children and has no child accounts, advertising or tracking.

12. Changes

If the app’s data handling changes, we will update this policy and its “Last updated” date before the change ships.

简体中文

1. 适用范围与开发者

Hushpage（Hushpage: Headache Diary）是由 Shanjie Han 开发的 iPhone 与 Apple Watch 头痛日记，包含主屏幕与锁屏小组件、控制中心控件以及 Siri 和快捷指令操作。本政策适用于以上全部内容。本页不表示应用已通过 Apple 审核或已经上架。

隐私与支持联系邮箱：hantmac@outlook.com。

2. 概要：开发者不收集任何数据

  • Hushpage 没有账号、无需登录，也没有服务器。你记录的任何内容都不会发送给开发者。
  • 不含广告、第三方分析、崩溃上报 SDK 或任何形式的跟踪。
  • 除非你主动分享，所有记录都只保存在你自己的设备上。

3. 应用保存哪些内容、保存在哪里

  • 你的记录：每条记录包含时间、头痛程度（轻度、中度、重度）或“已用药”标记，以及你可选填写的备注。记录保存在 iPhone 上应用自身的容器中，仅与同一台 iPhone 上 Hushpage 的小组件和控件共享，以便它们显示和记录。
  • 设置：是否写入健康 App、报告时间范围、报告上可选填写的姓名等偏好保存在本机。
  • Apple Watch：使用手表 App 时，近期记录通过 Apple 的 WatchConnectivity 框架在你配对的 iPhone 与 Apple Watch 之间同步。传输只在你自己的设备之间进行，不经过开发者的任何服务器。
  • 备份：与其他应用数据一样，记录可能根据你自己的备份设置包含在 iCloud 或电脑备份中，由 Apple 按其政策处理。

4. Apple 健康（可选，默认关闭）

开启“Save to Apple Health”后，Hushpage 会请求写入头痛样本（含你选择的程度）到健康 App 的权限。Hushpage 不请求读取任何健康数据，也从不读取。写入健康 App 的数据由 Apple 和你在健康 App 中管理，你可以随时在那里删除，或在“设置 › 健康 › 数据访问与设备”中撤销 Hushpage 的权限。开发者不会将健康数据用于广告，也不会与任何人共享。

5. 报告与导出

医生报告（PDF）和 CSV 导出都在你的 iPhone 上生成，只有在你主动分享时（如隔空投送、邮件、信息或存储到“文件”）才会离开设备。接收方以及你用来分享的应用或服务将按其自身条款处理该副本。

6. Siri、快捷指令、小组件与控件

通过 Siri、快捷指令、操作按钮、小组件或控制中心记录时，运行的是 Hushpage 自己在本机上的操作，写入同一份本地记录。你对 Siri 说出的请求由 Apple 按其隐私政策处理。

7. 购买

一次性购买的“Doctor Reports”由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理，开发者不会收到付款资料。应用在本机通过 StoreKit 检查购买状态以解锁报告。

8. 支持邮件与本网站

当你发邮件联系支持时，我们会收到你选择发送的内容（如邮箱地址、问题描述、应用与 iOS 版本及附件），仅用于回复和解决问题。请不要发送包含你不想公开的健康信息的截图。

本政策和支持页托管于 GitHub Pages。访问这些页面会产生普通网页请求，托管方可能保留连接日志。这与应用本身无关，应用不会自行打开本网站。

9. 删除数据

  • 在应用中删除单条记录：点开某一天，左滑记录，或打开记录后点 Delete Entry。
  • 删除全部数据：从 iPhone 和 Apple Watch 上删除应用即可移除应用保存的记录；你自己备份中的副本按你的备份设置处理。
  • 已写入健康 App 的头痛样本会保留，直到你在健康 App 中删除。
  • 没有账号，也没有服务器副本，因此开发者这边没有可删除的数据。如需处理你发给我们的支持邮件，请联系 hantmac@outlook.com。

10. 健康信息与医疗用途

Hushpage 是个人日记，不是医疗器械，不提供诊断、治疗或医疗建议。你记录的信息属于你本人，是否分享以及与谁分享由你决定。

11. 儿童

Hushpage 不面向儿童，没有儿童账号、广告或跟踪。

12. 政策变更

如果应用的数据处理方式发生变化，我们会在变更发布前更新本政策及“最后更新”日期。
