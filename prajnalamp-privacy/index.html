PrajnaLamp Privacy Policy

适用范围 / Scope

本政策适用于由 JeremyShan 提供的般若心灯（PrajnaLamp）Android 应用，包名 com.hanshanjie.prajnalamp。隐私联系邮箱：hantmac@outlook.com。

This policy applies to the PrajnaLamp Android app provided by JeremyShan, package com.hanshanjie.prajnalamp. Privacy contact: hantmac@outlook.com.

本机记录 / Local records

应用将计数、诵读记录、书签、收藏、功课、愿文、设置及下载音频保存在本机。愿文可能包含您自行输入的个人信息或宗教信仰相关信息；它们不由本应用自动上传给开发者。应用不要求注册账号，不接入广告、行为分析或开发者自建云同步服务。

Chant counts, reading history, bookmarks, favorites, practice plans, vows, settings and downloaded audio are stored locally. Vows may contain personal information or information about religious beliefs that you choose to enter; the app does not automatically upload these records to the developer. No in-app account registration, advertising SDK, behavioral analytics or developer-operated cloud sync is used.

网络和第三方 / Network and third parties

内置经文和音频可离线使用。您选择在线播放或下载时，应用会向曲目来源发出 HTTPS 请求；当前在线曲目来源包括 drfiles.cturls.net，重定向可能涉及其分发服务。对方会收到 IP 地址及所请求的音频等网络信息，其日志与保留期限由对方决定，开发者无法保证其不记录数据。应用没有在音频请求中附加您的愿文、书签或功课记录。

Bundled texts and audio work offline. Streaming or downloading sends HTTPS requests to the audio source, currently including drfiles.cturls.net, and potentially its distribution services through redirects. The provider receives network information such as your IP address and requested audio. Its logging and retention are controlled by that provider; we cannot guarantee that it keeps no logs. The app does not attach your vows, bookmarks or practice records to audio requests.

购买与 Google Play / Purchases and Google Play

付费下载由 Google Play 处理，应用不直接接收您的银行卡号或付款密码。Google 可能按照其自身政策处理交易、应用交付、安全、诊断及商店使用数据，开发者可能通过 Play Console 获得订单或汇总报告。处理范围以您使用的 Google 服务及其隐私说明为准。

Paid downloads are processed by Google Play. The app does not directly receive payment card numbers or payment passwords. Google may process transaction, delivery, security, diagnostic and store-use data under its policies; the developer may receive order or aggregate reports through Play Console. Refer to the privacy notices for the Google services you use.

备份与分享 / Backup and sharing

只有当您主动导出或分享时，选中的内容才会交给您选择的文件位置或接收应用。导出的 JSON 备份没有加密，可能包含完整愿文及修行记录；请保存到可信位置。分享图片临时存入应用缓存。所选云盘、聊天工具或其他应用的处理由其自身政策决定。本应用未启用系统自动云备份，也不提供 iCloud 导入。

Export and sharing occur only when you choose them and send the selected content to the destination or app you select. JSON backups are unencrypted and may include your full vows and practice records; store them securely. Shared images are temporarily held in the app cache. Cloud storage, messaging and other receiving apps apply their own policies. This app does not enable automatic system cloud backup or provide iCloud import.

权限 / Permissions

通知用于您开启的佛历提醒，以及系统支持的媒体播放通知。网络用于在线音频、下载和您主动打开的外部链接。振动用于拨珠反馈；媒体前台服务和唤醒锁用于播放。应用不申请联系人、定位、麦克风或相册读取权限。文件导入导出通过 Android 系统选择器由您指定位置。

Notifications support optional calendar reminders and system media playback notifications. Network access supports online audio, downloads and external links you choose to open. Vibration provides bead feedback; the media foreground service and wake lock support playback. The app does not request contacts, location, microphone or photo-library read permissions. File import and export use the Android system picker with a location you select.

保存与删除 / Retention and deletion

本机记录通常保存至您修改、删除、清除应用存储或卸载应用。可在应用中删除愿文、功课、书签及下载音乐；清除应用存储或卸载会移除本机应用数据。已经导出或分享的副本不会随卸载删除，需要您在目标位置另行处理。若通过邮件联系开发者，您提供的邮件地址、内容和附件将用于回复及处理问题，并仅按处理请求及适用法律所需期限保存。可通过联系邮箱请求删除您发送给开发者的信息，但依法需保留的部分除外。

Local records normally remain until you edit or delete them, clear app storage or uninstall. Vows, plans, bookmarks and downloaded music can be removed in the app. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes local app data, but not exported or shared copies; delete those separately at their destinations. If you email the developer, your email address, message and attachments are used to respond and handle the issue, and retained only as needed for that purpose and applicable legal obligations. You may email a request to delete information you sent to the developer, subject to required legal retention.

儿童、选择和安全 / Children, choices and security

本应用并非专为儿童设计，不主动索取儿童个人信息。您可以关闭提醒、仅使用离线内容，并避免在愿文或邮件中填写敏感个人信息。系统应用沙箱和 HTTPS 用于降低风险，但不能保证绝对安全。若您认为有儿童通过邮件提交了个人信息，请联系开发者处理。

The app is not specifically designed for children and does not actively request their personal information. You can disable reminders, use offline content and avoid entering sensitive personal information in vows or email. The Android app sandbox and HTTPS reduce risks but do not guarantee absolute security. Contact the developer if you believe a child has submitted personal information by email.

政策更新 / Updates

我们可能因功能或法律要求更新本政策，并在本页注明更新日期。如以后引入账号或云同步，将相应更新隐私说明及必要的同意流程。

This policy may be updated for functionality or legal requirements, with the revision date shown on this page. If accounts or cloud sync are introduced, the privacy information and necessary consent flow will be updated accordingly.
