StayAwake（醒着）Support / 技术支持

Last updated / 最后更新：2026-11-02

简体中文 · English · 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

简体中文

联系支持

支持邮箱：hantmac@outlook.com

StayAwake（醒着）是适用于 macOS 14 或更新版本的原生菜单栏防休眠工具，支持 Apple 芯片与 Intel Mac。中文与英文、浅色与深色外观跟随系统设置。

本页分别说明 Mac App Store 版与独立官网版；提供支持页面不代表商店版已经上架或通过审核。

两个版本有什么区别？

项目 Mac App Store 版 官网版
开盖时防止空闲休眠、屏幕控制、定时结束 支持 支持
独立合盖保护 不提供，遵循 Apple 支持的外接显示器合盖使用条件 可选实验性功能，需要管理员批准签名辅助服务
购买与更新 由 Apple 管理，不需要官网激活码 独立官网购买与许可证
试用与设备规则 按实际 App Store 产品及 Apple 规则 7 天试用，买断许可证供同一用户同时使用两台个人 Mac
退款 向 Apple 申请，资格由 Apple 决定 购买后 14 天内可申请全额退款

不同渠道的购买不会自动互通。官网版的试用、两台授权及 14 天退款不是商店版承诺。

开始使用

  1. 打开应用，点击菜单栏的单色猫头鹰图标。
  2. 开启「防止休眠」，让 Mac 在开盖时不因键盘或鼠标空闲而睡眠。
  3. 需要屏幕常亮时开启相应选项；不需要时关闭它，普通防休眠仍可继续。
  4. 选择预设时长、自定义时长，或持续到你手动停止。
  5. 任务完成后关闭主开关或退出应用，释放本应用的防休眠断言。

实心猫头鹰表示本应用持有防休眠断言，空心表示关闭；它不是任务、网络或 VPN 正常运行的证明。每次重新启动应用时，保护默认关闭。

定时、息屏与登录启动

  • 修改运行中的时长会从现在重新计时。到期、关闭主开关或退出应用会释放本应用的断言。
  • 「立即息屏」请求所有已连接屏幕进入睡眠，不等于停止普通防休眠；其它应用仍可能重新点亮屏幕。
  • 手动锁屏与系统安全策略仍然有效。不要为了让屏幕常亮而关闭安全保护。
  • 可在面板设置登录启动与 Dock 图标显示。macOS 可能要求在系统设置中批准登录项；登录启动不代表自动开启防休眠。
  • 低电量或较高热压力会结束会话。请保持 Mac 通风，不要把仍在运行的 Mac 放进包里。

为什么合盖后任务还是停止？

Mac App Store 版仅防止开盖时的空闲休眠，不覆盖合盖休眠，也不安装管理员辅助服务。 若要合盖使用外接显示器，请遵循 Apple 针对你的 Mac、供电和外接设备规定的受支持条件；本应用不会绕过这些条件。

官网版提供的独立合盖保护仍为实验性功能，需主动启用并批准签名辅助服务。它可能暂时影响全局系统休眠（包括手动睡眠），结束后需要恢复。长期合盖、供电切换及应用卡死恢复等场景尚未完成全面实机验收，不能承诺在所有 Mac 和环境下有效。请先在自己的设备上验证，不要关闭 SIP、绕过 Gatekeeper 或忽略 macOS 安全警告。

任何版本的防休眠都不保证互联网、Wi-Fi、VPN 或远程服务器持续可用。

Mac App Store 版：购买、重新下载与退款

商店版采用一次付费下载，无订阅、无 App 内购买、无官网激活码。实际价格与可用状态以 App Store 为准，购买、安装与更新由 Apple 管理。

重新下载时，请使用原购买 Apple 账户。账单或退款问题可前往 Apple 报告问题，是否符合退款条件由 Apple 决定。无需通过官网 Paddle 付款或安装管理员辅助服务。

仅官网版：购买、激活与更换 Mac

  • 官网：StayAwake
  • 购买许可证：付款由 Paddle 处理。
  • 找回激活码：使用原购买邮箱。
  • 官网版退款政策：购买后 14 天内可联系支持，或通过 Paddle 收据及 Paddle 买家支持申请退款。

官网版提供 7 天完整试用及一次买断授权，供同一用户同时使用两台个人 Mac。首次激活需要联网；已激活版本可离线使用，并在联网时进行授权校验。更换 Mac 前，在许可证界面停用旧设备；若旧设备已无法使用，请联系支持协助。

不要在公开评论、截图或问题报告中展示完整激活码。App Store 购买不等同于官网许可证。

排查常见问题

应用打开后没有普通窗口： 这是菜单栏应用，请点击菜单栏猫头鹰图标。若使用菜单栏管理工具或屏幕空间有限，请检查图标是否被隐藏。

屏幕熄灭但任务仍在继续： 屏幕休眠与系统空闲休眠是不同功能。需要画面常亮时开启相应选项；仅做下载或渲染时可以让屏幕休息。

仍然进入休眠： 检查主开关、定时是否到期、电量及温度，并区分空闲休眠、手动睡眠与合盖休眠。其它应用、系统策略及管理员配置仍可能影响行为。

停止会话后 Mac 仍不睡眠： 本应用只释放自己持有的断言，其它应用也可能阻止睡眠。可在“活动监视器”的“能耗”中检查；熟悉终端的用户也可运行只读命令 pmset -g assertions 查看当前断言。若提供输出，请先遮挡用户名、进程参数等个人资料。

下载或 VPN 中断： 防休眠不是网络保活保证，请同时检查网络、VPN 客户端和远程服务状态。

安全停止与卸载

Mac App Store 版： 关闭会话，关闭登录启动，退出后删除应用。此版本没有管理员辅助服务需要移除。

官网版： 先在「帮助与安全」中恢复正常休眠并移除辅助服务，再按需停用许可证，关闭登录启动并退出应用，最后删除应用。

若官网版提示恢复失败，请保持 Mac 开盖、接电并通风，保留应用和辅助服务以便其继续重试，然后联系支持。必要时可在终端执行下面的恢复命令；它需要管理员授权，会更改全局休眠设置，仅用于官网版辅助服务恢复排查，商店版不需要此步骤：

Getting started

  1. Launch StayAwake and click the monochrome owl in the menu bar.
  2. Enable Prevent sleep to prevent idle sleep while your Mac is open.
  3. Enable Keep display on if needed; otherwise let the display rest while ordinary sleep prevention continues.
  4. Choose a preset, a custom duration, or keep the session running until you stop it.
  5. Turn off the main switch or quit when finished to release this app’s assertions.

A filled owl means this app holds a sleep-prevention assertion; an outline means it is off. It does not prove that your task, internet connection or VPN is healthy. New launches start with protection off.

Timers, displays and launch at login

  • Editing a running duration restarts the timer from now. Expiry, switching protection off or quitting releases the app’s assertions.
  • Turn displays off now requests sleep for all connected displays without ending ordinary sleep prevention. Other apps may wake them again.
  • Manual locking and system security policies remain effective. Do not disable security protections just to keep a display on.
  • Launch at login and Dock visibility are optional panel settings. macOS may require approval in System Settings; launch at login does not automatically start protection.
  • Low battery or high thermal pressure ends a session. Keep your Mac ventilated and never place a running Mac in a bag.

Why does a task stop when I close the lid?

The Mac App Store edition prevents idle sleep while the Mac is open. It does not override lid sleep or install an administrator helper. For closed-display use with an external monitor, follow Apple’s supported conditions for your Mac, power supply and attached equipment. StayAwake does not bypass them.

The website edition’s independent closed-lid protection is experimental and requires explicit activation and approval of a signed helper. It may temporarily affect global system sleep, including manual Sleep, and must restore it afterwards. Long-duration lid use, power switching and app-hang recovery have not completed comprehensive physical acceptance testing. Effectiveness on every Mac and setup is not guaranteed. Test your own setup first; do not disable SIP, bypass Gatekeeper or ignore macOS security warnings.

Neither edition guarantees continuous internet, Wi-Fi, VPN or remote-server availability.

Mac App Store purchases, redownloads and refunds

The Store edition is a paid download with no subscriptions, in-app purchases or website license key. The actual App Store listing determines price and availability. Apple manages purchases, installation and updates.

Use the original purchasing Apple Account to redownload. For billing or refunds, visit Apple’s Report a Problem; Apple determines eligibility. No Paddle checkout or administrator helper is required.

Website edition only: purchase, activation and moving Macs

  • StayAwake website
  • Buy a license: payments are processed by Paddle.
  • Recover a key: use the original purchase email.
  • Website refund policy: within 14 days of purchase, contact support or request through your Paddle receipt or Paddle buyer support.

The website edition offers a 7-day full trial and a one-time license for two personal Macs used simultaneously by the same person. First activation requires internet. Activated copies can work offline and validate when online. Deactivate an old Mac in the license interface before moving; contact support if the old device is unavailable.

Never publish full license keys in comments, screenshots or bug reports. An App Store purchase is not a website license.

Troubleshooting

No ordinary window after launching: StayAwake is a menu-bar app. Click the owl. If you use menu-bar management software or have limited menu-bar space, check whether the icon is hidden.

Display off but work continues: display sleep and system idle sleep are different. Enable Keep display on for visible presentations, or allow the display to rest for background tasks.

The Mac still sleeps: check the main switch, timer expiry, battery and temperature. Distinguish idle sleep from manual Sleep and lid sleep. Other apps, system policies and administrator configuration may also affect behavior.

The Mac will not sleep after stopping: StayAwake releases only its own assertions; other apps may still prevent sleep. Check Activity Monitor’s Energy view. Terminal users can inspect assertions with the read-only command pmset -g assertions. Redact usernames, process arguments and other personal information before sharing output.

A download or VPN disconnects: sleep prevention is not network keepalive. Check your network, VPN client and remote service separately.

Safe stopping and removal

Store edition: stop the session, disable launch at login, quit, then remove the app. There is no administrator helper to uninstall.

Website edition: restore normal sleep and remove the helper in Help & safety, deactivate the license if needed, disable launch at login, quit, then remove the app.

If the website edition reports restoration failure, keep your Mac open, powered and ventilated. Leave the app/helper installed so it can retry and contact support. Reporting a problem

Email hantmac@outlook.com with:

  • App version/build and distribution channel (Store or website);
  • Mac model/chip and macOS version;
  • Lid open/closed, AC/battery and external-display setup;

  • Reproduction steps, expected result and actual result;
  • Optional redacted screenshots or minimal diagnostic information.

Never send passwords, verification codes, full license keys, payment-card details or private desktop contents. Support will not ask for them. This page does not automatically collect diagnostic reports; you choose what to send.
