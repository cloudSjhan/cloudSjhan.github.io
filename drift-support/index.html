Last updated / 最后更新：2026-10-27

Drift 提供 iPhone、iPad 和 Mac 版本。请按平台阅读：iOS/iPadOS 使用触控手势和本地恢复区；以下 Mac 快捷键、Finder 废纸篓与官网版许可证说明仅适用于 Mac。

Drift is available for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Choose the appropriate platform section: iOS/iPadOS uses touch controls and an on-device recovery vault; the Mac keyboard, Finder Trash and website-license instructions below apply only to Mac.

Drift for iPhone / iPad — 帮助与支持

适用于 iOS/iPadOS 17 或更新版本。Mac 快捷键与独立官网版许可证操作不适用于本节。

支持邮箱：hanshanjieapp@gmail.com
隐私政策：https://cloudsjhan.github.io/drift-privacy-policy/

开始使用

  1. 首页选择“照片图库”或“打开文件夹”。
  2. 图库通过系统申请权限；可使用有限访问。文件夹通过“文件”选择器打开，可来自设备、iCloud Drive 或可用的外置存储。
  3. 按需设置照片/视频/混合、排序方式与每组数量，再开始浏览。
  4. 打开整理开关后才在浏览中标记待删。完成一组或点“复核”，先保留误选项目，再确认移除。

照片权限与云端内容

  • 权限可在系统“设置”的 App/照片权限中更改；不同系统版本的具体路径可能不同。
  • 有限权限只能看到你选择开放的项目；可使用 App 内的管理入口或系统设置调整。
  • 仅在 iCloud 的照片、视频或实况照片需要网络和下载时间。打开项目或显式导出时可能下载；网格/相邻预取不会主动请求原图下载。
  • 连接不稳定时可取消或重试，切到后台或锁屏会暂停/释放播放和相关加载。自动降级可能减少预取或自动动态播放。
  • 文件夹打不开时重新通过“文件”选择器授权，确认外置盘已连接、云盘文件可下载；重新打开相同目录不会新增重复最近记录。

收藏和导出

收藏在本地保留原件引用，不移动原件，也不会自动变成系统“照片”的收藏或跨设备同步收藏。取消收藏不删除照片。

通过系统分享面板分享/保存原件副本。配对媒体可能导出多个文件。若原件移动、设备断开或权限改变，先恢复访问；文件原件可重新定位。图库项目失效时检查照片权限，不能用选择一个不相关文件来替代图库原件。

删除、恢复与卸载注意事项

  • 标记不是删除。确认前可撤销，在复核页面点选救回；系统删除取消/失败后，未成功的项目继续保留复核。
  • 图库项目遵循 Apple 删除确认与“最近删除”规则，请在系统“照片”中恢复。iCloud 删除可能同步到其他设备。
  • 文件夹项目进入 Drift 的本地恢复区。可从“统计 → 恢复区”尝试还原、保存副本，或单独确认永久删除。原位置与权限必须可用，不覆盖已存在原文件。
  • 恢复区仍使用设备空间，进入“最近删除”也不保证立即释放空间；统计不将未真正释放的字节夸大为腾出空间。
  • 卸载前请先恢复或导出恢复区的重要原件。卸载可能清除 App 内保存的唯一文件；恢复区不是长期备份。
  • RAW/JPEG、Live Photo 等可能包含配对文件，确认前请备份并核对整组。

购买、恢复下载与退款

iPhone/iPad 版本为一次付费下载，无订阅、无内购、无激活码。当地价格以 App Store 当前展示为准。Apple 管理付款、下载、更新和退款；重新下载时使用原购买 Apple 账户。退款或账单问题可访问 https://reportaproblem.apple.com/ 。

不要将官网版 Mac 的两台设备许可证、试用/激活流程套用到 iOS。不同平台或渠道的购买是否通用，取决于实际 App Store 产品配置；本帮助页不承诺通用购买或跨渠道授权。

内存、播放与诊断

低电量、温度升高或内存压力下，App 可能降低缓存、关闭预取或停止自动动态播放；这是资源保护，不代表原件丢失。可在条件允许时主动播放。避免在电量极低或设备过热时长时间下载/整理大量原件。

设置中的“诊断信息”可生成本地快照，记录内存/CPU 时间、缓存、请求计数与资源状态等，不包含照片 ID、文件名、原始路径或图像。报告不会自动上传，需要你主动分享。快照不能代表完整电池续航测试。

报告问题

联系支持时请提供：

  • Drift 版本/构建号、iPhone/iPad 型号、iOS/iPadOS 版本；
  • 来源是照片图库、iCloud Photos、iCloud Drive、本地文件夹还是外置存储；
  • 媒体格式，问题是否只发生在云端项目或某段视频；
  • 复现步骤、预期与实际行为；
  • 是否开低电量模式、是否发热，以及自愿提供的脱敏截图或诊断报告。

不要发送 Apple 密码、验证码、API 私钥、私人原片或银行卡资料。支持人员不会通过邮件要求这些信息。

Drift for iPhone / iPad — Support

Requires iOS/iPadOS 17 or later. Mac keyboard shortcuts and website-edition activation instructions do not apply to this section.

Contact: hanshanjieapp@gmail.com
Privacy policy: https://cloudsjhan.github.io/drift-privacy-policy/

Getting started and controls

Open Photos Library or Open a Folder, authorize access in the system prompt/picker, then choose a media filter, order and group size.

  • Tap once to show/hide controls, including when tapping black letterboxing.
  • Double-tap to save a favorite; repeating this does not remove it. The heart button can toggle a favorite.
  • Swipe up/down for next/previous. Swipe right to favorite; swipe left to mark only when Tidy is on.
  • Undo the last mark/favorite change before confirming deletion.
  • Pinch in or tap the date to open That Day. Videos and GIFs support this too. Use Select and density options for batch actions.
  • Show controls to pause/play video, scrub or mute. Press and hold during playback for temporary fast playback.

Photos, video and GIFs preserve their full original aspect ratio without cropping, stretching or automatic zoom. Black letterboxing is expected when the media ratio differs from the display. iPad and landscape layouts adapt the controls and grid instead of stretching media.

Permissions, iCloud and files

Photos access, including Limited Access, can be changed in system Settings. Only allowed assets are visible. Cloud-only originals need a connection and time to download; opening/exporting can download, while grid/prefetch requests do not proactively request original downloads. Cancel/retry on a poor connection. Backgrounding/locking stops playback and related work; resource protection may reduce prefetch/autoplay.

For folders, reconnect drives or reselect through Files if access expires. Ensure cloud files are available. Reopening the same folder updates a single recent entry rather than duplicating it.

Favorites and sharing

Favorites are local references, not moves, backups or automatic Apple Photos favorites/cross-device sync. Removing a favorite does not delete its original. System sharing prepares copies; companion media may produce multiple files. Reconnect or reauthorize an unavailable source. File originals can be relocated; Photos originals require appropriate Photos access.

Deletion and recovery

Marking is not deletion. Review first and rescue items; cancellation/failure retains the remaining review.

  • Photos deletion follows Apple’s confirmation and Recently Deleted rules. Recover through Photos. iCloud changes may synchronize elsewhere.
  • Folder originals move to Drift’s on-device recovery vault. From Statistics → Recovery vault, attempt restoration, save a copy, or explicitly confirm permanent deletion. Restoration needs the destination and access; existing files are not overwritten.
  • Recently Deleted and the vault still occupy storage; neither is a guarantee of immediately freed space.
  • Restore/export important vault items before uninstalling Drift. Uninstalling may remove the only remaining originals. The vault is not a long-term backup.
  • RAW/JPEG and Live Photo items may include companions; back up and check all originals before removal.

Purchase, resources and contacting support

The iPhone/iPad edition is a paid download without subscriptions, in-app purchases or activation codes. The local App Store listing determines the price. Apple handles billing, downloads, updates and refunds; use the original Apple Account to redownload and https://reportaproblem.apple.com/ for billing/refund requests. Do not apply website Mac license/device limits to iOS or assume universal purchase across platforms/channels.

Low Power Mode, thermal or memory pressure may reduce caches/prefetch/autoplay. You may explicitly play when conditions allow. Diagnostics generates local memory/CPU/cache/request/resource snapshots, not photo IDs, filenames, original paths or image content. It is shared only at your choice and is not a battery-life benchmark.

Email the contact above with version/build, device and OS, media source/format, reproduction steps and expected/actual results. Optional screenshots and diagnostics should be de-identified. Never send Apple passwords, verification codes, API private keys, payment-card data or private originals.

Mac — English

Welcome to Drift

Drift is a photo and video browser for macOS 14 or later. Browse a folder or the Photos library one item at a time, revisit a day, and mark unwanted items for a separate review before deleting.

Contact: hanshanjieapp@gmail.com

Getting started

  1. Open Drift and select Open a Folder, or Photos Library.
  2. Choose Mixed, Photos or Videos; pick an order and group size.
  3. Scroll up/down or use the arrow keys to browse.
  4. If you want to tidy, turn on Tidy mode with D. Swipe left or press the left arrow/Delete key to mark an item. Nothing is deleted yet.
  5. Open the basket or finish the group, review marked items, rescue any you want to keep, then confirm.

Browse orders

Seven orders are available: Random, Date Newest First, Date Oldest First, Size Largest First, Size Smallest First, Name A–Z and Name Z–A. Ordered groups skip viewed items until a new round starts.

Files use available file/capture dates. EXIF dates are refined and cached in the background, so first-scan ordering may initially use file timestamps. Photos library sizes are estimates based on available metadata; they are not exact byte sizes. Name ordering falls back to capture date when a library asset has no accessible filename.

Folders, external drives and SD cards

Choose a folder through the macOS picker to grant permission. Subfolders are included; hidden files and library/package bundles are skipped. Do not select the inside of a .photoslibrary package: use Photos Library instead.

If a recent folder no longer opens, pick it again. Ensure an external disk or SD card is mounted. First scanning, preview reading and sharpening large images depend on storage speed. The app shows available embedded previews before the full image and recognizes matching RAW + JPEG camera files as a combined item where supported.

Photos and iCloud permissions

Allow Drift in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos. Check that the library you want is available as the system Photos library. Cloud-only assets require an internet connection and time to download. iCloud Drive access is granted by selecting the corresponding folder.

Deletion, recovery and backups

  • Marking is not deletion. Review the batch before confirming.
  • Folder files go to Trash where macOS and the selected volume support it. Recovery is through Finder Trash, not Drift’s undo after confirmation.
  • Photos library deletion uses Apple’s confirmation and Recently Deleted behavior.
  • iCloud deletions can synchronize to your other devices.
  • A combined RAW + JPEG or Live Photo item can include multiple files. Check carefully and back up valuable originals first.

App Store purchase and website licenses

Mac App Store edition: paid upfront, no subscription, no license key and no trial activation screen. Apple manages downloads, billing and updates. Reinstall from the Apple Account used to buy the app. For payment/refund questions, use Apple’s Report a Problem.

Separate website edition: seven-day trial and a one-time license for two personal Macs. Activation requires an internet connection. Use Drift → License to activate or deactivate a Mac. Buy at hhdrift.com/en/buy/; the key appears after payment and is emailed to you. Recover a lost key at hhdrift.com/en/recover/. Full refunds within 14 days: refund policy, or email support@hhdrift.com. An App Store purchase is not automatically a website license, and vice versa.

Favorites you can return to

Open Favorites from Home, the viewer toolbar, the end-of-group screen, or ⇧⌘F. Favorites stay available after groups finish and after relaunching Drift. They are a local index: Drift does not silently move or duplicate large originals, and does not synchronize hearts to Finder tags or Apple Photos favorites.

Select favorites to preview, copy original files, reveal them in Finder, remove them from the collection, or Export to Folder. Export creates copies, never moves originals, and does not overwrite existing destination files. RAW/JPEG or Live Photo companion files stay paired where supported. Removing a favorite does not delete its original.

If a disk is disconnected, permissions expire, or an original moves, the record stays in the collection. Reconnect the drive or use Locate Original; for a pair, select all matching files together. Old ID-only favorites from earlier builds are retained and recover their metadata when the source is opened again.

Hints and reading time

Use View → Show Keyboard Hints While Browsing or the viewer’s display menu to enable shortcut guidance. It defaults to on and remembers your choice. Hints fade in when the pointer moves and fade out after 3 seconds without movement; keyboard browsing alone does not bring them back. Temporary feedback stays for 5–10 seconds based on its length; hover to pause dismissal.

History and privacy

Review history, favorites and statistics are stored locally. Resetting viewing history/statistics preserves saved favorites and source photos. See the privacy policy for the differences between editions.

Report a problem

Email hanshanjieapp@gmail.com with:

  • Drift version/build and macOS version;
  • source type (folder, SD card, iCloud Drive or Photos library);
  • file format, if relevant;
  • steps to reproduce, expected behavior and actual behavior;
  • an optional screenshot with personal information removed.

Do not send passwords, full license keys, card details or private media. We will ask for a safe, minimal diagnostic example if needed. No account sign-in is required for browsing.

随时找回收藏

首页、浏览工具栏、每组完成页和 ⇧⌘F 均可打开“收藏”。收藏在结束浏览或重新启动后仍会保留。默认保存本地索引，不悄悄移动或复制大体积原片，也不会把爱心同步到访达标签或系统“照片”收藏。

选中后可大图回看、复制原文件、在访达中显示、取消收藏，或“导出到文件夹”。导出只创建副本、不移动原片、不覆盖已有文件；支持的 RAW/JPEG 和实况配对文件保持配对。取消收藏不会删除原文件。

磁盘未连接、权限失效或原片移动时，记录仍保留。请重新连接磁盘或使用“重新定位原文件”，配对媒体需一起选择完整文件。旧版本中仅保存 ID 的收藏不会丢弃，重新打开原来源后会补全信息。

快捷键提示和阅读时间

通过“显示 → 浏览时显示快捷键提示”或浏览器画面显示菜单控制提示，默认开启且记住选择。鼠标移动时提示淡入，停止移动 3 秒后淡出；只用键盘浏览不会重新唤出提示。临时操作反馈按长度显示 5–10 秒，鼠标悬停时暂停消失倒计时。
