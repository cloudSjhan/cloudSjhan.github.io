QuickPeek Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Last updated / 最后更新：2026-11-13

English · 简体中文 · Support / 技术支持

English

1. Scope and contact

This policy covers the QuickPeek setup app and its macOS Quick Look extension, maintained by Shanjie Han under the QuickPeek product name. It describes the current implementation. This page does not indicate App Review approval or App Store availability.

Privacy questions and support: hantmac@outlook.com.

2. No developer-side collection or tracking

QuickPeek has no account, advertising, analytics service, developer-operated file server or third-party crash-reporting SDK. The app and extension do not upload your file contents, file names, paths, settings or local diagnostic reports to the developer. They do not track you across apps or websites.

The app and extension use macOS App Sandbox. They do not request Full Disk Access, global keyboard monitoring, Accessibility or screen-recording permission. Separate developer test tools may use automation permissions; they are not bundled in the product.

3. Files you preview

When Finder asks QuickPeek to preview a folder or ZIP, QuickPeek reads directory or archive information needed for that preview. With Expand All on, this includes additional folder levels within the selected container, subject to resource limits. Ordinary file contents are read or rendered only after you select a supported file. Your original file names and contents are not translated or edited.

Preview processing happens on your Mac. File and image/PDF data may be held temporarily in process memory while a preview is open. QuickPeek does not maintain a developer-accessible index of your files or automatically extract archives to a persistent workspace. Files in cloud-synced folders remain subject to macOS and your storage provider; the system may download a file when you access it, independently of QuickPeek.

4. Preferences and local diagnostics

The Expand All choice is stored locally in the extension’s preferences.

The current version writes local diagnostic events in its sandbox’s Application Support directory and to the macOS logging system. A sandbox report includes a random per-session ID, event times and outcomes, app/OS versions, process ID and sandbox status. It does not record file names, paths, file contents or screenshots. Each report retains at most 200 events; the current version does not automatically rotate reports across sessions. These reports are not automatically transmitted.

macOS may separately handle system diagnostics or backups according to your own settings and Apple’s policy.

5. Purchases

The planned paid download is processed by Apple through the App Store. QuickPeek does not receive your payment-card or bank details. App Store transactions, receipts and any information Apple provides to developers are governed by Apple’s services and policies. This page is not a claim that sales have begun.

6. Support email and this website

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send, including your email address, message and any attachments. We use it to respond and investigate the issue. Please avoid private documents and redact sensitive file names from screenshots. Contact the same address to ask about deletion of support correspondence, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

This website is separate from the app. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses the existing blog theme, which may load third-party assets and visitor-count services. Visiting it can reveal ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and browser metadata, to the host and those services. The website’s behaviour must not be confused with the app’s lack of analytics or tracking. The app does not open these pages automatically.

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Privacy Statement

7. Retention and deletion

Closing the preview ends the current browsing session. Preferences and local diagnostic reports may remain in QuickPeek’s sandbox containers. Removing the app may not immediately remove all containers, system logs or backup copies; macOS and your backup settings control those lifetimes. We do not remotely retain or delete your local files. Contact support if you need guidance on removing QuickPeek’s own local data without affecting other apps.

There is no QuickPeek account or server-side file copy to delete. Your original documents are not deleted by uninstalling QuickPeek.

8. Changes

If QuickPeek’s data handling changes, this policy and its date will be updated before the change is distributed.

简体中文

1. 适用范围与联系方式

本政策适用于 QuickPeek 设置 App 及其 macOS 快速查看扩展，由 Shanjie Han 以 QuickPeek 产品名称维护，说明当前实现的数据处理方式。本页面不表示应用已通过 Apple 审核或已经上架。

隐私与支持联系邮箱：hantmac@outlook.com。

2. 不向开发者收集或跟踪数据

QuickPeek 无账号、广告、分析服务、开发者文件服务器或第三方崩溃上报 SDK。App 与扩展不会向开发者上传文件内容、文件名、路径、设置或本机诊断报告，也不会跨 App 或网站跟踪你。

App 与扩展使用 macOS App Sandbox，不请求完全磁盘访问、全局键盘监听、辅助功能或录屏权限。开发者的独立测试工具可能使用自动化权限，但不包含在产品中。

3. 你预览的文件

Finder 请求预览文件夹或 ZIP 时，QuickPeek 读取该次预览所需的目录或压缩包信息。开启展开全部后，会在资源预算内读取所选容器的更多目录层级；普通文件只有在你选中支持的文件后才读取或渲染正文。原始文件名与正文不会被翻译或修改。

处理在你的 Mac 上完成。预览期间，文件及图片／PDF 数据可能暂存于进程内存；QuickPeek 不建立开发者可访问的文件索引，也不自动解压到持久工作区。云同步文件夹中的文件仍受 macOS 和存储服务管理，系统访问文件时可能自行下载，这与 QuickPeek 向开发者传输数据不同。

4. 偏好与本机诊断

展开全部的选择保存在扩展的本机偏好中。

当前版本会在自身沙盒的 Application Support 目录和 macOS 日志系统记录本地诊断事件。沙盒报告包含随机会话 ID、事件时间和结果、App／系统版本、进程 ID 及沙盒状态，不包含文件名、路径、正文或截图。每份报告最多保留 200 条事件；当前版本尚未自动跨会话轮转报告。报告不会自动发送给开发者。

macOS 可能按你的设置及 Apple 政策单独处理系统诊断或备份。

5. 购买

计划中的付费下载由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理。QuickPeek 不接收你的银行卡或银行信息。商店交易、收据及 Apple 向开发者提供的信息受 Apple 服务与政策约束。本页不表示应用已经开始销售。

6. 支持邮件和本网站

你发送支持邮件时，我们会收到你主动提供的邮箱地址、消息和附件，仅用于回复和排查问题。请不要发送私人文档，并遮盖截图中的敏感文件名。如需询问删除支持通信，请联系同一邮箱；适用的法定保留义务除外。

本网站与 App 分开。页面托管于 GitHub Pages，沿用现有博客主题，可能加载第三方资源和访问计数服务。浏览页面时，主机及相关服务可能接收 IP 地址、浏览器信息等常规连接数据。不要将网站行为与 App 无分析／跟踪服务混为一谈。App 不会自动打开这些网页。

  • Apple 隐私政策
  • GitHub 隐私声明

7. 保留与删除

关闭预览会结束当前浏览会话。偏好与诊断报告可能仍保留在 QuickPeek 沙盒中。删除 App 不一定立即删除所有沙盒容器、系统日志或备份副本，其保留时间由 macOS 和备份设置决定。我们不会远程保留或删除你的本机文件。需要清理 QuickPeek 自身数据时可联系支持，避免影响其他应用。

QuickPeek 无账号或服务器端文件副本可供删除；卸载 QuickPeek 不会删除你的原始文档。

8. 政策变更

如数据处理方式发生变化，会在相关版本分发前更新本政策及日期。
