Drift — Photo & Video Browser for Mac / Mac 照片与视频浏览器

Let your memories drift. / 让照片，再次流动。

Drift is a native macOS photo and video browser. Rediscover a folder or your Photos library one item at a time, then tidy gently with marks, undo and a separate deletion review.

Drift 是为 Mac 设计的照片与视频浏览器。沉浸浏览文件夹或“照片”图库，逐张回顾、按自己的节奏轻松整理；先标记、可撤销，最后统一复核。

Browse, remember, tidy / 浏览、回忆、整理

  • Immersive photo and video feed / 沉浸式照片与视频流
  • Local folders, SD cards, external drives and Photos library / 文件夹、SD 卡、外置盘与照片图库
  • Seven browse orders / 七种排序，随机或按日期、大小和名称浏览
  • That Day: pinch in to see media captured on the same day / 双指捏合，回到那天
  • Live Photo playback / 实况照片播放
  • Mark first, review before deleting / 先标记，再复核删除
  • Keyboard and trackpad controls / 原生键盘与触控板操作
  • Local history and favorites, no advertising / 本地记录与收藏，无广告

Requirements / 系统要求： macOS 14.0 or later / macOS 14.0 或更新版本。

Mac App Store edition / 商店版： paid upfront, no subscription or license key. Availability is determined by the App Store listing after approval. / 付费下载，无订阅、无激活码；具体可用状态以审核后的商店页面为准。

Release status / 发布进度： The Mac App Store release is in preparation. A download link will be added after approval. / Mac App Store 版正在准备发布，审核通过后将提供下载入口。
