Drift Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Last updated / 最后更新：2026-10-27

Drift 提供 iPhone、iPad 和 Mac 版本；不同平台及分发渠道的购买、权限与删除机制分别说明。iPhone/iPad 请阅读本页对应章节，以下 Mac 条款仅适用于 Mac。

Drift is available for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Purchase, permission and deletion behavior is described separately for each platform and distribution channel. Please use the iPhone/iPad sections for iOS/iPadOS; the Mac terms below apply only to Mac.

iPhone 与 iPad 版本隐私说明

本节适用于通过 Apple App Store 分发的 Drift iOS/iPadOS 版本。原有 Mac App Store 与独立官网版条款仍分别适用；iOS 版本不使用官网版激活码或第三方更新服务。

开发者：Shanjie Han。隐私联系邮箱：hanshanjieapp@gmail.com。

1. 访问照片与文件

Drift 仅访问你通过系统授予权限的照片图库内容，以及你通过系统“文件”选择器选择并授权的文件夹。你可以使用系统支持的有限照片访问，并在系统设置中更改或撤销权限。App 为显示、播放、排序、按日期归组和整理，读取媒体及所需元数据，例如拍摄/文件日期、尺寸、视频时长和可获得的文件大小。

图库与 iCloud 照片由 Apple PhotoKit 提供，iCloud Drive 及其他文件位置由相应系统或存储服务管理。打开仅在云端的媒体、导出原件或访问尚未下载的文件时，可能产生向 Apple 或你选择的存储服务的网络请求，并消耗流量；系统同步和下载受相应服务政策约束。这不等于开发者接收到你的照片。

Drift 不将照片、视频、媒体元数据、浏览历史或收藏上传至我们运营的服务器。 预览生成、浏览和整理逻辑在你的设备上执行。首页内置装饰照片随 App 提供，不代表读取或上传你的相册。

2. 设备上的存储

App 在本机保存偏好设置、最近目录的授权引用、安全作用域书签、收藏引用及元数据、浏览历史与整理统计。为显示、播放或分享，可能创建本地预览和临时媒体副本；资源缓存可能由 App 或系统清理。

收藏通常保存原件的本地引用，不代表上传、备份、移动原件或同步到系统“照片”的收藏。取消收藏或重置统计不会删除源照片，也不等于清空全部 App 数据。重新定位失效的文件引用需要你重新授权。

iOS/iPadOS 版本的文件恢复区可能保存你已明确确认移出的原文件及其配对文件，以及用于恢复的原位置、日期和授权引用。恢复区不是云端备份，仍占用设备空间；具体行为见下一节。App 数据是否进入系统备份由 Apple、设备设置及你所使用的存储服务管理，Drift 不提供开发者托管的云备份服务。

3. 删除、恢复与 iCloud 同步

标记待删不会立刻删除媒体。只有在你复核并确认后，App 才会执行移除；确认前可以取消标记或撤销相应操作。

  • 系统照片图库：通过 Apple 的权限与删除确认流程处理，恢复按系统“最近删除”的规则进行，不由 Drift 保证期限或成功。
  • 从“文件”选择的文件夹：确认移除后，文件进入 Drift 设备内的恢复区，而不是 Mac 的 Finder 废纸篓。你可以在恢复区尝试还原到原目录、保存副本，或再次明确确认永久删除。恢复需要相应位置和权限可用；原文件不会被直接覆盖。
  • 删除 App 前请先检查恢复区：卸载 Drift 可能删除其中保存的唯一原件及 App 数据，无法依靠 Drift 再找回。请事先恢复或导出重要内容。
  • 跨设备同步：在 iCloud 照片、iCloud Drive 或其他同步位置删除或移出文件，可能同步影响其他设备和云端。RAW/JPEG 或 Live Photo 配对可能包含多个文件。

进入系统“最近删除”或 Drift 恢复区不代表立即释放存储空间。请先备份重要原件，并仔细核对待删列表。

4. 分享、导出与诊断

只有当你主动选择分享/导出时，Drift 才准备原件副本，并调用系统分享面板或你选择的目标。接收内容的 App、云盘或其他服务按其政策处理这些副本。取消或完成分享后，App 会尝试清理临时文件；系统也可能管理或保留相关临时数据。

诊断快照在设备上生成，可包含采样时间、App 进程内存/CPU 时间、缓存预算和使用量、媒体请求总量/失败/取消计数、低电量状态、温度状态、前后台状态和模拟器标记。当前内置诊断报告不包含照片 ID、文件名、原始路径或图像内容，不会自动发送给开发者；只有你主动通过分享或邮件发送时，相应接收者才会取得你选择发送的内容。

5. 购买、跟踪与账号

iOS/iPadOS 版本为一次付费下载，无订阅、无 App 内购买、无第三方激活码。下载、付款、退款和更新由 Apple 管理，适用 App Store 的条款。浏览无需创建 Drift 账号；我们不会通过浏览功能取得你的 Apple 账户密码或银行卡资料。

App 不包含广告、第三方分析或跨 App 跟踪 SDK，不为广告或行为画像自动采集使用数据。系统本身及你使用的第三方存储或分享服务可能依据其各自政策处理数据。

6. 支持与权利请求

你主动联系支持时，我们会收到你选择发送的邮箱、问题描述、版本信息和附件，用于回复和排查。请不要发送密码、API 密钥、完整付款信息或私人照片；必要时可使用去除个人信息的示例。

如需询问本政策、请求处理你主动发送的支持资料，或说明隐私问题，请联系 hanshanjieapp@gmail.com。我们会根据适用法律及必要的留存义务处理。政策和支持页的普通网页访问由网站托管方处理；参阅 Apple 隐私政策 和 GitHub 隐私声明。

Privacy information for iPhone and iPad

This section applies to Drift for iOS/iPadOS distributed through the Apple App Store. The existing Mac App Store and separate website-edition terms continue to apply to their respective editions. The iOS edition does not use website license keys or third-party update services.

Developer: Shanjie Han. Privacy contact: hanshanjieapp@gmail.com.

1. Access to Photos and files

Drift accesses only the Photos assets allowed by your system authorization and folders you select and authorize through the system Files picker. Limited Photos access is supported where available; you can change or revoke permission in system Settings. Displaying, playing, sorting, grouping and tidying media requires reading media and relevant metadata, such as capture/file dates, dimensions, video duration and available file-size information.

Apple PhotoKit provides Photos and iCloud Photos access. Apple and your selected storage providers manage iCloud Drive and other file locations. Opening cloud-only media, exporting originals or accessing files that have not downloaded can cause network requests to Apple or your selected provider and use data. Those services govern their own downloads and synchronization; this does not mean the developer receives your photos.

Drift does not upload your photos, videos, media metadata, viewing history or favorites to a server operated by us. Preview generation, browsing and tidying take place on your device. The decorative Home photographs are bundled artwork, not evidence of access to or upload of your personal library.

2. Storage on your device

Drift locally stores preferences, recent-folder authorization references and security-scoped bookmarks, favorite references and metadata, viewing history and aggregate tidying statistics. Local previews and temporary media copies may be created for display, playback or sharing. The app or operating system may clear caches.

Favorites normally reference originals; they are not a cloud upload, backup, move or synchronization with Apple Photos favorites. Removing a favorite or resetting statistics does not delete source media or erase all app data. Locating an unavailable original requires renewed authorization.

The iOS/iPadOS recovery vault can hold original files and companion files you explicitly confirmed moving out of a selected folder, together with original locations, dates and authorization references needed for restoration. It is not a cloud backup and still occupies device storage. Apple, device settings and your storage services govern system backups; Drift does not provide developer-hosted cloud backup.

3. Deletion, recovery and synchronization

Marking is not immediate deletion. Removal happens only after review and confirmation; before confirmation, you can rescue items or undo the corresponding action.

  • Photos library: removal uses Apple’s authorization and system confirmation flow. Recovery follows the system’s Recently Deleted rules; Drift does not guarantee a recovery period or successful recovery.
  • Folders selected in Files: confirmed originals move to Drift’s on-device recovery vault, not Finder Trash. You can attempt restoration to the original directory, save a copy, or separately confirm permanent deletion. Restoration requires the original location and access to remain available and does not overwrite an existing file.
  • Before uninstalling Drift: restore or export important vault items. Uninstalling can remove the only remaining originals and app data; Drift cannot recover them afterwards.
  • Synchronization: deleting or moving content in iCloud Photos, iCloud Drive or another synchronized location may affect the cloud and other devices. RAW/JPEG and Live Photo items may contain multiple files.

Recently Deleted and the recovery vault do not immediately free storage. Back up valuable originals and check the review list carefully.

4. Sharing, export and diagnostics

Only after you explicitly choose sharing/export does Drift prepare copies and open the system share sheet or your chosen destination. Receiving apps and storage providers handle those copies under their own policies. Drift attempts to clean temporary copies after completion or cancellation; the operating system may also manage or retain temporary data.

Diagnostic snapshots are generated locally and can include a sampling timestamp, process memory/CPU time, cache usage and budget, request/failure/cancellation counts, Low Power Mode, thermal state, foreground state and a simulator indicator. The built-in report does not contain photo identifiers, filenames, original paths or image content and is not automatically sent to the developer. Recipients receive information only when you explicitly share or email it.

5. Purchases, tracking and accounts

The iOS/iPadOS edition is a paid download with no subscriptions, in-app purchases or third-party activation keys. Apple manages purchases, downloads, refunds and updates under App Store terms. Browsing does not require a Drift account; the browsing features do not receive Apple Account passwords or payment-card details.

The app includes no advertising, third-party analytics or cross-app tracking SDKs and does not automatically collect usage data for advertising or profiling. Apple and storage/share services you use may process information under their respective policies.

6. Support and requests

When you contact support, we receive the email address, issue description, version information and attachments you choose to provide, solely for responding and troubleshooting. Do not send passwords, API keys, full payment details or private media; use a de-identified example where possible.

For questions, privacy concerns or requests concerning support information you voluntarily sent, contact hanshanjieapp@gmail.com. We handle requests as required by applicable law and necessary retention obligations. Ordinary visits to the policy/support website are handled by its host; see the Apple Privacy Policy and GitHub Privacy Statement.

Mac — English

Scope and developer

Drift is a macOS photo and video browser developed by Shanjie Han, distributed through the Mac App Store and, separately, through the Drift website. This policy distinguishes those editions because their purchase and update mechanisms differ.

Privacy contact: hanshanjieapp@gmail.com.

Photos, videos and folder access

Drift accesses only folders you select and the Photos library access you grant through macOS. It reads media and relevant metadata to display, sort and group items, including capture or file dates, dimensions, duration and file sizes where available. The app uses Apple PhotoKit for your Photos library and iCloud Photos. iCloud downloads and synchronization are handled by Apple and your configured storage providers.

Drift does not upload your photos, videos, capture metadata, viewing history or favorites to a server operated by us. Processing and image preview generation happen on your Mac. Granting Photos access does not give us remote access to your library.

Local storage

The app stores preferences, recent-folder and saved-favorite security-scoped bookmarks, review history, favorite media references and metadata, aggregate review/deletion statistics and cached capture dates locally. Image previews and the motion portion of Live Photos may be cached on your Mac to support playback. App Store builds do not create a Drift account, trial record or third-party license activation record.

Removing a recent folder or resetting review history in the app does not delete source media or erase saved favorites. Exporting favorites creates copies only in a folder you choose. macOS manages the app container and Keychain independently; uninstalling the app does not necessarily erase every cache, preference or Keychain item.

Deletion and iCloud

Marking an item for deletion does not immediately remove it. After you review and confirm, folder files are moved to macOS Trash where supported. Photos library assets are deleted through Apple’s system confirmation flow and may be recoverable in Recently Deleted under Apple’s rules.

Changes to an iCloud Photos library or iCloud Drive folder may synchronize to other devices. A RAW + JPEG pair or a paired Live Photo may include more than one file. Back up important media and check the confirmation screen before deleting.

Mac App Store edition

The Mac App Store edition is a paid-upfront app with no subscription, third-party license activation, or third-party updater. Purchases and updates are managed by Apple. Drift does not receive your card details, Apple ID credentials or App Store purchase history for its browsing functions.

The App Store edition includes no advertising, third-party analytics, behavioral profiling or cross-app tracking SDKs. We do not collect data from the app for advertising or analytics. Photos accessed locally are not sent to us for storage or analysis.

Website edition only: trial, licensing and updates

The separate website edition offers a seven-day trial and a one-time license. Trial dates, license keys, license-instance identifiers and related entitlement information are stored locally, including in macOS Keychain. These entries may survive app reinstallation.

Paddle (Paddle.com Market Ltd.) sells the website edition as Merchant of Record and processes payment, tax, receipts and refunds under its own terms; we never receive card numbers. Online activation, validation and deactivation contact Drift’s license service at hhdrift.com (hosted on Cloudflare) with the license key and a randomly generated install identifier, to enforce the two-Mac limit. The Mac’s name, photos and usage are not sent. The service stores one-way hashes of keys and device identifiers, the order ID, Paddle customer ID and a keyed hash of the purchase email; license and recovery emails are sent through Resend. Contact support@hhdrift.com to request deletion.

The website edition uses Sparkle for update checks. Update and download requests may expose standard connection information, such as IP address and request metadata, to the website host. These website-edition services are not part of the Mac App Store edition.

Third-party policies:

  • Paddle Privacy Policy
  • Resend Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Privacy Statement — hosting of this policy and support website

Support messages and websites

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, app/OS version and issue description. We use it to respond and troubleshoot. Please do not email private photos, passwords, full license keys or payment-card information unless there is a clear, agreed need for a particular non-sensitive diagnostic item. Support correspondence is separate from automatic in-app data collection.

This policy and support page are hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting a website or following an external link causes ordinary web requests to its host; that is distinct from Drift uploading your photo library. External platforms apply their own policies.

Children, requests and changes

Drift is not a service directed specifically at children and does not add child-specific accounts, advertising or tracking. If you believe you have sent personal information in a support request that should be removed, contact us. We will respond as applicable law requires, subject to legal record-retention obligations.

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The current policy and its revision date will remain on this page.

Mac — 简体中文

适用范围与开发者

Drift 是由 Shanjie Han 开发的 macOS 照片与视频浏览器，分别通过 Mac App Store 和 Drift 官网分发。两种版本的购买及更新方式不同，本政策分别说明。

隐私联系邮箱：hanshanjieapp@gmail.com。

照片、视频与文件夹权限

Drift 仅访问你选择并授权的文件夹，以及你通过 macOS 授权的“照片”图库。为显示、排序和按日期归组，App 会读取媒体及相关元数据，例如拍摄或文件日期、尺寸、时长，以及可获得的文件大小。系统图库和 iCloud 照片通过 Apple PhotoKit 提供；云端下载与同步由 Apple 及你配置的存储服务处理。

Drift 不会把你的照片、视频、拍摄元数据、浏览历史或收藏上传到我们运营的服务器。 浏览处理和预览生成在 Mac 上进行。授予图库权限不会让开发者远程访问你的图库。

本地保存的内容

偏好设置、最近文件夹及收藏原文件的安全作用域书签、回顾历史、收藏引用及媒体元数据、回顾及删除统计、拍摄日期索引均保存在本机。为支持显示和播放，App 可能缓存图片预览或实况照片的动态部分。App Store 版不会创建 Drift 账号、试用记录或第三方激活记录。

移除最近文件夹或重置浏览记录不会删除原照片，也不会清空已保存的收藏。导出收藏仅在你选择的文件夹中创建副本。App 容器与钥匙串由 macOS 分别管理；卸载 App 不一定会删除所有缓存、偏好设置或钥匙串条目。

删除与 iCloud 同步

“标记删除”不会立刻删除媒体。复核并确认后，本地文件在系统支持时移至 macOS 废纸篓；图库资产通过 Apple 系统确认流程删除，能否从“最近删除”恢复以 Apple 的规则为准。

iCloud 照片图库或 iCloud Drive 文件夹的删除可能同步到其他设备。 RAW + JPEG 或配对的实况照片可能包含多个文件。整理重要资料前请备份，并核对确认列表。

Mac App Store 版

Mac App Store 版采用付费下载，无订阅、无第三方激活码、无第三方应用更新器。购买和更新由 Apple 管理。Drift 的浏览功能不会接收你的银行卡信息、Apple ID 凭据或 App Store 购买历史。

App Store 版不包含广告、第三方分析、行为画像或跨 App 跟踪 SDK；我们不会为了广告或分析自动收集 App 数据。本地访问照片不代表把照片提交给开发者存储或分析。

仅官网版：试用、授权与更新

独立的官网版提供七天试用及买断授权。试用日期、激活码、设备实例标识和相关授权状态保存在本机，包括 macOS 钥匙串；重新安装后部分记录仍可能保留。

官网版由 Paddle（Paddle.com Market Ltd.）作为 Merchant of Record 销售，按其条款处理付款、税费、收据和退款，我们不接触银行卡号。联网激活、校验和停用会连接 hhdrift.com 上的 Drift 授权服务（托管于 Cloudflare），发送激活码和随机生成的安装标识，用于执行“最多 2 台 Mac”的限制；不发送 Mac 名称、照片或使用记录。服务器保存激活码和设备标识的单向哈希、订单号、Paddle 客户编号及购买邮箱的加密哈希；激活码与找回邮件通过 Resend 发送。如需删除，请写信至 support@hhdrift.com。

官网版使用 Sparkle 检查更新。下载与更新请求可能向网站托管服务暴露 IP 地址和请求元数据等连接信息。以上官网版服务不属于 Mac App Store 版。

相关第三方政策见英文部分的 Apple、Paddle、Resend、Cloudflare 与 GitHub 隐私政策链接。

支持邮件与网页访问

如果你联系支持，我们会收到你主动发送的邮箱、App/系统版本、问题描述等信息，仅用于回复和排查。请不要主动发送私人照片、密码、完整激活码或银行卡资料。支持邮件与 App 自动采集属于不同场景。

本隐私政策与支持页托管在 GitHub Pages。访问网页和外部链接会向相应网站发起普通网络请求，这不等于 Drift 上传了你的照片库；外部平台适用各自的政策。

儿童、数据请求与政策变更

Drift 并非专门面向儿童的服务，不设置儿童专属账号、广告或跟踪。如果你在支持邮件中提供了希望删除的个人信息，可以联系上述邮箱；我们会依据适用法律处理，并遵守必要的法定记录保留义务。

政策随产品及服务变化更新，最新版本和修订日期会保留在本页面。
