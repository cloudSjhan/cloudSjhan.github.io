StayAwake（醒着）Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Last updated / 最后更新：2026-11-02

简体中文 · English · 技术支持 / Support

简体中文

1. 适用范围与开发者

StayAwake（醒着）是由 Shanjie Han 开发的 macOS 菜单栏防休眠工具，提供 Mac App Store 版与独立官网版。两种版本的购买、授权和系统权限不同，本政策分别说明；本页不表示应用已通过 Apple 审核或已经上架。

隐私与支持联系邮箱：hantmac@outlook.com。

2. Mac App Store 版：不收集个人数据

本节适用于 bundle ID 为 com.hantmac.StayAwake.Store 的 Mac App Store 版本。

  • 不需要创建 StayAwake 账号，不包含广告、第三方分析、行为画像或跨 App 跟踪 SDK。
  • 不向开发者上传任务内容、电脑名称、设备标识或使用记录，不包含官网版的 Paddle 付款或激活码授权接口。
  • 持续时长、Dock 显示、首次启动等偏好保存在应用自己的本地设置中。系统运行时间仅用于计时和安全判断，不用于设备指纹识别。
  • 防休眠断言、电池及温度状态检查均在本机进行，不会因此向开发者传输数据。
  • 不安装管理员辅助服务，不需要辅助功能、屏幕录制、相机或麦克风权限。可选的登录启动由 macOS 管理。

商店版的购买、下载、更新和退款由 Apple 处理，适用 Apple 的条款与隐私政策。我们不会通过应用的防休眠功能取得你的 Apple 账户密码或银行卡资料。Apple 可能依据你的系统设置和自身政策处理商店及系统诊断信息，这与 StayAwake 自动向开发者收集数据不同。

3. 仅官网版：试用、许可证与在线服务

官网版与 Mac App Store 版分别发行，以下服务不属于商店版：

  • 本地授权信息：试用日期、激活码、随机安装标识及相关授权状态可保存在本机，包括 macOS 钥匙串。删除或重新安装应用不一定清除这些记录。
  • 授权请求：激活、校验与停用会连接 hhdrift.com 上的 StayAwake 授权服务，发送激活码和随机生成的安装标识，用于管理授权及同时使用的设备数量。不发送任务内容或电脑名称。
  • 服务器记录：授权服务保存激活码和设备标识的哈希、订单编号及购买邮箱的加密哈希等必要授权记录。
  • 付款与邮件：Paddle 处理付款、税费、收据与退款；Resend 用于发送授权及找回邮件。我们不接触完整银行卡号。为完成付款或邮件投递，相应服务商会处理你提交的购买资料和收件邮箱。
  • 网站及下载：Cloudflare 托管官网、授权服务和安装包。网页、下载及授权请求可能产生 IP 地址、请求时间及其他常规连接或安全日志，由相关服务商按照其政策处理。

官网版不包含广告或分析脚本，不采集任务内容或电脑名称。它并非完全离线的产品：使用在线购买、授权、找回或网页功能时会发生相应网络请求。

4. 仅官网版：可选辅助服务与本地诊断

官网版的实验性独立合盖保护需要你明确批准安装签名的管理员辅助服务。辅助服务通过本机通信处理防休眠、恢复及安全状态，不代表给予开发者远程访问权限。它会读取完成该功能所需的供电、电量、温度和系统休眠状态，并可能将恢复、错误及状态信息写入 macOS 本地统一日志。

这些本地检查及日志不等于自动向开发者发送诊断报告。只有你主动通过邮件或其他方式提供排查资料时，我们才会收到你选择发送的内容。Mac App Store 版不包含此辅助服务或独立合盖保护。

5. 支持邮件与网页访问

当你主动联系支持时，我们会收到你选择发送的邮箱、问题描述、应用与系统版本、附件等资料，仅用于回复、排查和处理相关请求。请不要发送密码、验证码、完整激活码、完整付款资料或含个人信息的桌面截图。

本隐私政策和技术支持页托管于 GitHub Pages。访问本网站会向网站托管方和页面所使用的资源服务发起普通网页请求，可能产生连接日志；点击外部链接后，目标服务适用自己的政策。这与应用本身的数据处理是不同场景。

相关服务商政策：

  • Apple 隐私政策
  • GitHub 隐私声明
  • Cloudflare 隐私政策
  • Paddle 隐私政策
  • Resend 隐私政策

6. 删除、隐私请求与政策变更

商店版没有需要申请删除的 StayAwake 服务器账号。你可以在本机管理应用偏好，并在系统设置中关闭登录启动；卸载不保证 macOS 自动删除所有偏好、缓存或钥匙串条目。

官网版卸载前，应先结束会话，恢复正常休眠并按应用提示移除辅助服务，再按需停用许可证、关闭登录启动并退出。请勿在恢复失败时直接删除辅助服务。详细步骤见技术支持页。

如需查询、更正或删除你主动提交的支持资料，或处理官网版的授权及购买关联信息，请联系 hantmac@outlook.com。我们会按照适用法律处理请求，并遵守必要的账务、安全及法定留存义务。Apple、Paddle 等服务商自行持有的资料，可能需要向相应服务商提出请求。

StayAwake 不设置儿童专属账号、广告或跟踪。若你认为支持通信中包含需要处理的儿童个人信息，请联系上述邮箱。

产品或服务变化时，我们可能更新本政策；最新内容及修订日期将保留在本页。

English

1. Scope and developer

StayAwake is a macOS menu-bar sleep-prevention utility developed by Shanjie Han. It has a Mac App Store edition and a separate website edition. Their purchase, licensing and system-permission mechanisms differ and are described separately below. This page does not indicate App Review approval or App Store availability.

Privacy and support contact: hantmac@outlook.com.

2. Mac App Store edition: no personal data collected by the app

This section applies to the Mac App Store edition, bundle ID com.hantmac.StayAwake.Store.

  • No StayAwake account, advertising, third-party analytics, behavioral profiling or cross-app tracking SDK is included.
  • The app does not upload task contents, computer names, device identifiers or usage records to the developer. It contains no website-edition Paddle checkout or license-key endpoint.
  • Duration, Dock visibility, first-launch state and similar preferences remain in the app’s local settings. System uptime is used only for timing and safety, not fingerprinting.
  • Sleep-prevention assertions and battery/thermal checks happen locally and do not transmit that information to the developer.
  • No administrator helper is installed. Accessibility, Screen Recording, camera and microphone permissions are not required. Optional launch at login is managed by macOS.

Apple handles App Store purchases, downloads, updates and refunds under its own terms and privacy policy. The app’s sleep-prevention features do not receive your Apple Account password or payment-card details. Apple may process store or system diagnostic information under its policies and your system settings; this is separate from automatic data collection by StayAwake.

3. Website edition only: trial, licensing and online services

The website and Mac App Store editions are separate distributions. The following services are not part of the Store app:

  • Local licensing data: trial dates, license keys, a random installation identifier and entitlement state may be stored locally, including in macOS Keychain. Removing or reinstalling the app does not necessarily erase these records.
  • License requests: activation, validation and deactivation contact the StayAwake license service at hhdrift.com, sending a license key and a randomly generated installation identifier to manage entitlements and simultaneous device limits. Task contents and computer names are not sent.
  • Service records: the licensing service stores necessary records such as hashes of license keys and device identifiers, order IDs and keyed hashes of purchase emails.
  • Payments and email: Paddle handles payments, tax, receipts and refunds. Resend delivers licensing and recovery emails. We do not receive full payment-card numbers. These providers process purchase details and recipient email addresses as needed to provide their services.
  • Website and downloads: Cloudflare hosts the website, licensing service and installation files. Website, download and licensing requests may generate IP addresses, timestamps and standard connection or security logs, handled under the relevant provider’s policy.

The website edition includes no advertising or analytics scripts and does not collect task contents or computer names. It is not an entirely offline product: using purchase, licensing, recovery or website features makes the corresponding network requests.

4. Website edition only: optional helper and local diagnostics

Experimental independent closed-lid protection requires your explicit approval to install a signed administrator helper. The helper communicates locally to manage sleep prevention, restoration and safety state; it does not give the developer remote access. It reads power-source, battery, thermal and system-sleep state needed for that feature and may write restoration, error and status information to macOS local unified logs.

These local checks and logs are not automatically submitted diagnostic reports. We receive troubleshooting information only when you choose to send it by email or another channel. The Mac App Store edition has neither this helper nor independent closed-lid protection.

5. Support correspondence and website visits

When you contact support, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, issue description, app/macOS versions and attachments. We use it to respond, troubleshoot and handle related requests. Do not send passwords, verification codes, full license keys, full payment details or screenshots containing personal information.

This policy and the support page are hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting this site makes ordinary web requests to the host and resource providers used by the pages and may generate connection logs. External links lead to services governed by their own policies. Website visits are separate from processing inside the app.

Provider policies:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Paddle Privacy Policy
  • Resend Privacy Policy

6. Deletion, privacy requests and changes

The Store edition has no StayAwake server account to delete. You can manage local preferences and disable launch at login in system settings. Uninstalling does not guarantee that macOS removes every preference, cache or Keychain entry.

Before removing the website edition, stop its session, restore normal sleep and remove the helper through the app, then deactivate the license if needed, disable launch at login and quit. Do not simply delete the helper if restoration fails. See Support for details.

To request access, correction or deletion of support information you voluntarily provided, or website-edition license/purchase-related records, contact hantmac@outlook.com. We handle requests under applicable law, subject to necessary accounting, security and statutory retention requirements. Requests about records held independently by Apple, Paddle or other providers may need to be directed to those providers.

StayAwake does not offer child-specific accounts, advertising or tracking. Contact us if support correspondence contains a child’s personal information that needs attention.

We may update this policy when the product or services change. The latest version and revision date will remain on this page.
