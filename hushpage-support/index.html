Hushpage Support / 技术支持

Last updated / 最后更新：2026-11-11

English · 简体中文 · Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

English

Contact

Email: hantmac@outlook.com

Hushpage is a one-tap headache diary for iPhone with iOS 18 or later and Apple Watch with watchOS 11 or later. It is optimized for iPhone Duo. The interface is in English and always dark, so it stays easy on the eyes during an attack.

Getting started

  1. Open Hushpage. The current month is shown as a diary page.
  2. When a headache starts, tap Mild, Moderate or Severe. Nothing else is asked.
  3. Tap Took medication when you take acute medication.
  4. The button you tapped shows Got it and a bar confirms what was saved. Logged something by mistake? Tap Undo in that bar within a few seconds.

Each day shows one mark: its size and colour show the worst level logged that day. A short slate dash under it means medication was taken.

Add, change or delete an entry

  • Tap any past day to see its entries.
  • To add one you forgot, pick a time under Add to this day, then tap a level or Meds.
  • Tap an entry to change its level or time, add a note, or delete it. You can also swipe an entry to delete it.

Log without opening the app

  • Lock Screen and Control Center: touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then add a Hushpage control (Mild, Moderate, Severe or Took medication) to a bottom slot. In Control Center, tap + and add the same controls. Each one logs with a single tap.
  • Lock Screen widget: shows today’s level and the last seven days. Tapping it opens the app.
  • Home Screen widget: tap a level or Meds right on the widget.
  • Siri and Shortcuts: say “Log a headache in Hushpage” or “Log medication in Hushpage”. You can assign the Log Headache shortcut to the Action button.
  • Apple Watch: open Hushpage on your watch and tap a level; a full-screen Got it confirms it, with Undo. Swipe up (or turn the Digital Crown) for Today, with counts and each entry (swipe left to delete), and again for this month. You can also add the complication to a watch face.

Timing a headache

With Time how long headaches last on (Settings, on by default), tapping a level starts the clock. The headache shows on the main screen, the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island with how long it has lasted. When it ends, tap It’s over there, on Apple Watch, with the Headache over control in Control Center, or say “My headache is over in Hushpage”. If it gets worse, tap the higher level and the same headache is updated. Forgot to end one? After 72 hours Hushpage asks when it ended; open that day to set the time. Turn timing off and each tap is a single moment instead.

Medications and monthly limits

In Settings › Medications, add what you take, from the common names or typed yourself, and choose its type: simple pain reliever, triptan, combination pain reliever or other. Each medication gets its own button (the first three on the main screen; the rest under More). Under the calendar, Hushpage shows how many days this month you took each type, against a limit. The starting limits are the ICHD-3 reference values (15 days a month for simple pain relievers, 10 for triptans and combination pain relievers); change them or turn them off, ideally with your doctor. Hushpage counts days only and never suggests what to take.

Year view

Tap the grid button in the toolbar to see the whole year: twelve small months, one mark per day, with headache, severe and medication days counted at the top. Tap a month to open it.

Doctor report

Open the … menu or the toolbar and choose Doctor report. Pick 1, 3, 6 or 12 months and optionally type your name. The first page lists headache days, severe days and medication days for each month, with each month’s calendar. The following pages list every entry. Tap Share PDF to send it, print it or save it to Files. CSV exports the same entries as a spreadsheet.

Doctor reports and CSV export are a one-time purchase. Logging, the calendar, widgets, controls and Apple Watch are free.

Purchases and restoring

  • Purchases are processed by Apple. To use your purchase on a new iPhone, open Doctor report or Settings and tap Restore purchase while signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • If Family Sharing is on, family members can use the purchase too.
  • For billing questions or refunds, visit Apple’s Report a Problem. Apple decides refund eligibility.

Apple Health

In Settings, turn on Save to Apple Health to add each headache to the Health app as a Headache symptom with its level. Hushpage only writes to Health and never reads from it. If you turned access off by mistake, go to Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Hushpage.

Your data

Your entries are stored on your iPhone and copied to your paired Apple Watch. There is no account and nothing is sent to the developer. Deleting the app deletes its entries, so export a CSV first if you want to keep a copy. Moving to a new iPhone with an iCloud or computer backup carries your entries over. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Troubleshooting

A widget or control doesn’t show my latest entry. Widgets refresh after each entry, but iOS sometimes delays them. Open Hushpage once and the widget will catch up.

My Apple Watch shows different days than my iPhone. Open Hushpage on both devices with Bluetooth on and the devices near each other. Entries logged on the watch reach the iPhone even if the iPhone app wasn’t open at the time.

Entries aren’t appearing in the Health app. Check that Save to Apple Health is on and that Hushpage has write access in the Health app. Entries logged from widgets, controls or the watch are saved to Health the next time you open Hushpage on your iPhone.

Restore purchase didn’t unlock reports. Make sure you’re signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account that made the purchase, then try again on a working connection.

Reporting a problem

Email hantmac@outlook.com with your iPhone or Apple Watch model, iOS or watchOS version, the Hushpage version (Settings, bottom of the page), what you did and what happened. Please leave out health details you’d rather keep private.

Hushpage is a diary, not a medical device. For medical questions, talk to your doctor.

简体中文

联系方式

支持邮箱：hantmac@outlook.com

Hushpage 是一款点一下就能记录的头痛日记，适用于 iOS 18 或更新版本的 iPhone 和 watchOS 11 或更新版本的 Apple Watch，并已针对 iPhone Duo 优化。界面为英文，始终为深色，发作时看屏幕也不刺眼。

开始使用

  1. 打开 Hushpage，当前月份以日记页的形式显示。
  2. 头痛开始时，点 Mild（轻度）、Moderate（中度）或 Severe（重度），不需要填写其他内容。
  3. 服用急性止痛药时，点 Took medication。
  4. 点过的按钮会显示 Got it，提示条会告诉你刚记录了什么。点错了？几秒内在提示条里点 Undo 撤销。

每天显示一个墨点，大小和颜色表示当天记录的最高程度；墨点下方的灰蓝短线表示当天用过药。

补记、修改或删除记录

  • 点任意过去的一天，查看当天记录。
  • 补记忘记的头痛：在 Add to this day 下选好时间，再点程度或 Meds。
  • 点某条记录可修改程度、时间、添加备注或删除；也可以左滑删除。

不打开应用也能记录

  • 锁屏与控制中心：长按锁屏，点“自定”，在底部位置添加 Hushpage 控件（Mild、Moderate、Severe 或 Took medication）；在控制中心点 + 也可添加。点一下即可记录。
  • 锁屏小组件：显示今天的程度和最近 7 天，点击打开应用。
  • 主屏幕小组件：直接在小组件上点程度或 Meds。
  • Siri 与快捷指令：说 “Log a headache in Hushpage” 或 “Log medication in Hushpage”。也可以把 Log Headache 快捷指令设为操作按钮。
  • Apple Watch：在手表上打开 Hushpage，点程度即可记录，会出现全屏 Got it 确认并可撤销。向上轻扫（或转动数码表冠）查看 Today（当天次数与每条记录，左滑删除），再向上是本月。也可以把复杂功能添加到表盘。

记录头痛持续多久

开启 Time how long headaches last（在设置里，默认开启）后，点一下程度就开始计时。主屏幕、锁屏和灵动岛会显示这次头痛已持续多久。结束时，在这些地方、Apple Watch、控制中心的 Headache over 按钮点 It’s over，或对 Siri 说”My headache is over in Hushpage”。变严重了，就点更重的程度，会更新同一次头痛。忘了结束？超过 72 小时后 Hushpage 会提示，打开那一天补填结束时间。关掉计时，每次点击就只记录一个时间点。

用药与每月上限

在 设置 › Medications 里添加你用的药，可从常用药名中选，也可以自己输入，并选择类型：普通止痛药、曲普坦、复方止痛药或其他。每种药都有自己的按钮（主屏幕显示前三种，其余在 More 里）。月历下方会显示本月每类药用了几天，以及与上限的对比。默认上限采用 ICHD-3 参考值（普通止痛药每月 15 天，曲普坦和复方止痛药每月 10 天），可以修改或关闭，建议和医生商量。Hushpage 只统计天数，不会建议你用什么药。

全年总览

点工具栏里的格子图标查看全年：12 个小月历，每天一个墨点，顶部统计全年的头痛天数、重度天数和用药天数。点某个月即可打开。

医生报告

在 … 菜单或工具栏中选择 Doctor report，选择 1、3、6 或 12 个月，可选填写姓名。第一页列出每月的头痛天数、重度天数和用药天数以及每月月历，后面几页列出全部记录。点 Share PDF 可发送、打印或存储到“文件”；CSV 会把相同记录导出为表格。

医生报告和 CSV 导出为一次性购买。记录、月历、小组件、控件和 Apple Watch 均免费。

购买与恢复

  • 购买由 Apple 处理。换新 iPhone 后，使用同一个 Apple 账户，在 Doctor report 或 Settings 中点 Restore purchase 即可恢复。
  • 开启家人共享后，家庭成员也可使用该购买。
  • 账单或退款问题请访问 Apple 报告问题，是否可以退款由 Apple 决定。

Apple 健康

在 Settings 中开启 Save to Apple Health，每次头痛都会以带程度的“头痛”症状写入健康 App。Hushpage 只写入、从不读取健康数据。如果误关了权限，请前往“设置 › 健康 › 数据访问与设备 › Hushpage”。

你的数据

记录保存在你的 iPhone 上，并同步到配对的 Apple Watch。没有账号，也不会发送给开发者。删除应用会同时删除其记录，如需保留请先导出 CSV。通过 iCloud 或电脑备份迁移到新 iPhone 时，记录会一并迁移。详见隐私政策。

常见问题

小组件或控件没有显示最新记录：每次记录后小组件都会刷新，但 iOS 有时会延迟。打开一次 Hushpage 即可同步。

Apple Watch 和 iPhone 显示的日期不一致：打开蓝牙，让两台设备靠近，在两边都打开一次 Hushpage。在手表上记录的内容即使当时 iPhone 应用没有打开，也会同步到 iPhone。

健康 App 里看不到记录：确认已开启 Save to Apple Health，并在健康 App 中允许 Hushpage 写入。通过小组件、控件或手表记录的内容，会在下次打开 iPhone 上的 Hushpage 时写入健康 App。

恢复购买后报告仍未解锁：确认 App Store 登录的是当初购买的 Apple 账户，并在网络正常时重试。

反馈问题

请发邮件至 hantmac@outlook.com，并注明 iPhone 或 Apple Watch 型号、iOS 或 watchOS 版本、Hushpage 版本（设置页底部）、你的操作和出现的结果。请不要附上你不想公开的健康信息。

Hushpage 是日记，不是医疗器械。医疗问题请咨询你的医生。
