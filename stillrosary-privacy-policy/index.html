Still Rosary Privacy Policy

Support

Still Rosary is an independent Catholic rosary app for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch. The app has no registration, advertising, analytics SDK, or developer-operated server. The developer does not receive the prayers, intentions, or records you enter in the app.

What stays on your device

Still Rosary stores prayer progress, completed rosaries, intentions and notes, saved verses, and preferences in its app storage. The Apple Watch app has its own local storage and works offline. Watch records, intentions, and progress do not sync with iPhone or iPad. Your device backup settings are separate from the app’s sync option.

Optional iCloud sync

Sync is off by default. If you turn on Settings → iCloud → Sync completed rosaries, the IDs, mystery sets, and start and completion times of completed rosaries are sent to the private iCloud database for your Apple Account. When a synced record is deleted, the app keeps a deletion marker containing its ID and deletion status so the entry does not return from another device. The developer does not have routine access to your private iCloud database.

Intentions and notes, links between intentions and records, unfinished progress, saved verses, and preferences are never included in this sync. Turning sync off stops future sync on that device and does not erase existing iCloud copies. To remove an individual synced record, delete it in Journal while sync is on and allow the change to sync.

Reminders, widgets, and sharing

The daily prayer reminder is off by default. If you enable it and allow notifications, iOS schedules a generic reminder on your device. It includes no intentions, notes, or prayer history. The Home Screen verse widget shows only bundled Scripture and its attribution.

Sharing a verse sends only the verse and its attribution to a destination you choose. A records export is unencrypted and may include private intentions and notes. It is only shared when you choose a destination. If you follow a source or help link, the destination website receives ordinary web requests.

Deleting local data

Use Settings → Data → Erase local data on iPhone or iPad, or Settings → Erase Watch data on Apple Watch, to clear that device’s Still Rosary data. Clearing iPhone or iPad data also turns off sync and cancels its reminder. This does not delete copies on other devices, in iCloud, or in backups or exports you have made.

Contact

If you email hantmac@outlook.com, we use the email and anything you choose to send to respond to your request. Please do not include private intentions or your records export. This page and the support page are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub’s Privacy Statement applies to that hosting, and Apple’s Privacy Policy applies to Apple services.
